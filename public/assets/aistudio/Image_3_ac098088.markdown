## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a cheerful, imaginative, and deeply encouraging play-and-learn world tailored for early learners (ages 3–8) and their parents/educators on Windows desktop and tablet touchscreens. 

The aesthetic is **Tactile Soft-Cartoon / Pillowy Bubble**:
- **Character & Emotion:** Joyous, welcoming, safe, and celebratory. Every interaction feels like pressing a soft bouncy toy or collecting a sweet treat.
- **Physical Metaphor:** Rounded squishy surfaces, layered floating clouds, chunky candy-coated action buttons with thick glossy highlights, and glowing golden star rewards.
- **Child-First Accessibility:** Oversized touch targets (minimum 64px on interactive controls), high visual contrast, intuitive color-coded navigation icons, and zero harsh angles or intimidating corporate layouts.
- **Visual Rhythm:** Playfully rhythmic, friendly, and non-cluttered. It embraces soft dimensional depth rather than flat utility, celebrating victories with popping particles and bouncy transitions.

## Colors

The color palette is anchored in an uplifting sky pastel canvas with bright candy accents:

- **Primary (`#38BDF8` - Sky Pastel Blue):** The primary brand anchor representing clear blue skies, safety, and open space. Used for header bars, primary navigation tabs, and default interactive frames.
- **Secondary (`#FB7185` - Strawberry Pink):** A high-energy, warm accent dedicated to immediate actions, high-priority buttons (e.g., "Play Now", "Start Game"), and celebratory elements.
- **Tertiary (`#FBBF24` - Sunny Honey / Star Gold):** The reward and celebration token. Applied to XP bars, achievements, star ratings, and celebratory badges.
- **Supporting Accents:**
  - **Emerald Mint (`#34D399`):** Indicates success, complete modules, and positive feedback loops.
  - **Sunny Orange (`#FB923C`):** Denotes creative crafts, puzzles, and secondary action calls.
  - **Lavender Dream (`#A78BFA`):** Used for storytelling, music, and quiet exploration modes.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F0F9FF` - Cotton Cloud Blue):** The soft tinted-white foundation that eliminates eye strain while maintaining the illusion of floating above clouds. Text uses deep comforting slate-navy (`#0F172A`) to ensure WCAG AAA legibility for early readers.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes extreme legibility, open counters, rounded geometry, and a friendly, bouncy demeanor:

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in **Plus Jakarta Sans** with heavy weights (700 Bold, 800 ExtraBold). Its friendly geometric curves evoke soft building blocks, making title banners cheerful and effortlessly legible from tablet arm's-length.
- **Body & Prompts:** Set in **Nunito Sans** (weights 600 SemiBold and 700 Bold). The naturally rounded terminals provide a warm, approachable character that guides early readers without visual harshness or visual fatigue.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Kept uppercase or heavy-weight title case with generous line heights and tracking to ensure comfortable reading for children developing phonics and sight-reading comprehension.

## Layout & Spacing

Designed for fluid responsiveness across Windows tablet touch devices (10"-12") and large family desktop monitors (1080p, 1440p, 4K):

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column responsive fluid grid bounded within a maximum content container of 1600px, centering gameplay canvases cleanly with cloud-filled decorative margins on ultra-wide screens.
- **Tactile Spacing Philosophy:** Generous spacing rules ensure children do not accidentally trigger adjacent buttons. Layout gaps between interactive cards never shrink below `1.25rem` (`space-md`).
- **Touch Areas & Ergonomics:** Bottom and side edges of the screen prioritize primary interaction zones to support two-handed tablet grips. Navigation rails dock to the left or bottom with chunky 64px–80px touch zones.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a cheerful **Layered Cartoon Depth & Soft Skeuomorphism** model instead of sterile realistic dropshadows:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Base Layer (Sky Canvas):** Soft, soothing gradient from `#E0F2FE` down to `#BAE6FD` featuring organic vector cloud silhouettes.
  - **Surface Container (Cloud Panels):** Pure opaque cloud-white (`#FFFFFF`) with thick bottom bevels (`0 8px 0 rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.15)`), giving panels the feel of solid foam play mats.
  - **Elevated Controls (Buttons & Tokens):** 3D squishy buttons featuring double-layer drops: a bottom edge base (4px to 6px solid darker tint matching the element's hue) paired with a diffused ambient glow (`0 12px 24px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`).
- **Active State Depth:** On touch or click, buttons physically depress by 4px (`transform: translateY(4px)`), collapsing their bottom edge base and providing instantaneous physical satisfaction.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts full pill-shaped and bubble curvature:
- **Level 3 (Pill-shaped):** Extreme organic roundness across all interactive containers, progress capsules, and cards. 
- Standard chips and small buttons carry complete pill radii (`9999px` / `1rem+`).
- Content cards and game portals utilize soft bubble boundaries (`2rem` / `rounded-lg` to `3rem` / `rounded-xl`).
- Hard 90-degree corners are completely avoided across the application to reinforce a gentle, touch-inviting, child-safe environment.

## Components

- **Chunky Bubble Buttons:**
  - Oversized (minimum height: 60px; large action: 76px) with bold white text and subtle interior top-gloss highlights (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(255,255,255,0.3) 0%, rgba(255,255,255,0) 100%)`).
  - Solid bottom shadow lip of 4px–6px in a darker shade of the button's background hue.
  - Primary button in Strawberry Pink (`#FB7185`), secondary actions in Emerald Mint (`#34D399`) or Sunny Orange (`#FB923C`).

- **Cloud Game Cards:**
  - Pure white pillowy containers (`rounded-xl`, 3rem curvature) outlined by an ultra-soft 3px border (`#E0F2FE`) and raised 8px bottom shelf.
  - Cards feature a rich, high-contrast illustrative banner, bold title label, and a playful 3-star rating tracker.

- **Reward Chips & Tags:**
  - Capsule-shaped pills (`roundedness: 3`) using bright pastel fills paired with saturated borders.
  - Accompanied by cheerful 3D iconography (e.g., golden stars, apples, pencils) pinned seamlessly to the left side of the pill.

- **Checkboxes & Toggle Switches:**
  - Checkboxes are styled as rounded bouncy bubble buttons containing a thick, playful checkmark icon.
  - Toggles use pill tracks with oversized, round candy handles that squish slightly on sliding.

- **Input Fields (Kid Search & PIN Entry):**
  - Tall fields (56px–64px) with rounded-lg edges, pale blue interior fill (`#F8FAFC`), thick friendly focus borders (`#38BDF8`), and large, legible numbers/letters.

- **Star Progress Meters:**
  - Thick, rounded capsule bar containing segmented candy segments with an animated smiling star at the leading edge that bounces as XP fills.
